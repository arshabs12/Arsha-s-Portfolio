# 🛒 Blinkit Retail Sales & Outlet Performance Dashboard

## 📌 Business Problem
Blinkit (*India's Last Minute App*) operates in a high-velocity quick-commerce environment where inventory allocation, outlet format efficiency, and consumer product preferences directly drive profitability. This project analyzes **8,523 retail product records** to uncover top-performing product categories, compare outlet tiers and store types, and identify actionable growth opportunities.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Features
* **Power BI Desktop:** Interactive KPI cards, dynamic metric selector buttons (*Avg Sales, Total Sales, No. of Items, Avg Rating*), custom bookmarks/field parameters, and DAX measures
* **Data Preparation:** Power Query for data structuring and metric aggregation

* **Average Sales per Item:** 141
* **Total Number of Items:** 8,523 (~9K)
* **Average Customer Rating:** 3.92 / 5.0

## 🔍 Key Insights & Findings
* **Supermarket Type 1 Dominates Revenue:** **Supermarket Type 1** generated **787,550** in total sales across **5,577 items**—accounting for **~65.5%** of overall revenue—followed by Grocery Store (151,939), Supermarket Type 2 (131,478), and Supermarket Type 3 (130,715).
* **Tier 3 Outlets Lead Location Sales:** **Tier 3** locations drove the highest sales at **472.13K**, outperforming **Tier 2** (393.15K) and **Tier 1** (336.40K).
* **Top Product Categories by Volume:** **Fruits and Vegetables** and **Snack Foods** led item volume (~1.2K items each), followed by **Household**, **Frozen Foods**, and **Dairy**.
* **Strong Consumer Preference for Low-Fat Items:** **Low Fat** products accounted for roughly two-thirds of total item volume (~6K items vs. ~3K Regular items), consistently outperforming Regular variants across Tier 2 and Tier 3 locations.
* **Outlet Size & Historical Stability:** **Medium-sized** outlets led total sales (**507.9K**) ahead of Small (**444.79K**) and High (**248.99K**) outlets. Annual establishment trends remained steady around ~130K–200K, with a noticeable dip around 2011.

## 💡 Business Recommendations
1. **Prioritize Supermarket Type 1 & Tier 3 Inventory:** Allocate faster replenishment cycles and high-demand stock (**Fruits & Vegetables, Snack Foods, Household**) to Tier 3 Supermarket Type 1 stores, which drive the bulk of company revenue.
2. **Scale Low-Fat Assortment Across Tiers:** Expand shelf placement and bundled promotions for **Low Fat** variants in Tier 1 and Tier 2 outlets to replicate the strong volume conversion seen in Tier 3.
3. **Investigate the 2011 Establishment Dip & Grocery Store Margins:** Audit store operations and assortment breadth in lower-volume formats (such as Supermarket Type 3 and Grocery Stores) to lift average customer ratings above 4.0.
